- [**Reconstructing single-cell resolution from spatial transcriptomics with CellRefiner**](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-70090-2) — 2026-02-27, *Nature Communications*

  `single-cell resolution reconstruction` `spatial transcriptomics` `physical model` `data integration` `cell-cell communication`

  Presents CellRefiner, a physics-based particle model that integrates scRNA-seq with paired spatial transcriptomics data to impute single-cell resolution from lower-resolution platforms. Models cells as particles connected by forces and optimizes locations using spatial proximity, gene expression similarity, and ligand-receptor constraints. Benchmarked across Visium, MERFISH, seqFISH, Slide-seqV2, and STARmap; improves contact-based cell-cell communication analysis in mouse cortex and lymph node tissues.

- [**Tumor cell villages define the co-dependency of tumor and microenvironment in liver cancer**](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-69797-z) — 2026-02-21, *Nature Communications*

  `spatial mapping` `deep learning` `tumor microenvironment` `tumor cell states` `spatial single-cell imaging`

  Develops a deep learning framework to spatially map tumor cell states and their surrounding microenvironmental architecture from spatial single-cell imaging and scRNA-seq across 2 million cells from 50 liver cancer specimens. Identifies discrete tumor cell "villages" each with unique molecular co-dependencies between tumor cells and their microenvironment, associated with patient outcomes. Validated in 740 liver cancer patients using single-cell, spatial, and bulk transcriptome data.
