- [**spRefine denoises and imputes spatial transcriptomics with a reference-free framework powered by genomic language model**](https://doi.org/10.1101/gr.281001.125) — 2026-02-03, *Genome Research*

  `denoising` `imputation` `genomic language model` `deep learning` `spatial transcriptomics`

  Introduces spRefine, a deep learning framework that leverages genomic language models to jointly denoise and impute spatial transcriptomic data without requiring a reference dataset. spRefine yields more robust cell- and spot-level representations after processing, substantially improving data integration. Enhances the accuracy of spatial ageing clock estimations and uncovers new aging-related relationships associated with neuronal function loss, offering new insights for analyzing ageing effects with spatial transcriptomics.

- [**Seq-Scope-eXpanded: spatial omics beyond optical resolution**](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-69346-8) — 2026-02-10, *Nature Communications*

  `tissue expansion` `sub-micrometer resolution` `spatial transcriptomics` `spatial proteomics` `platform`

  Presents Seq-Scope-X, which combines sequencing-based spatial transcriptomics with tissue expansion to surpass the 200–300 nm optical resolution limit of imaging-based methods. In liver tissue, the approach resolves nuclear and cytoplasmic compartments in nearly every single cell. Can also be adapted for spatial proteomics, profiling hundreds of barcode-tagged antibody stains at microscopic resolutions, establishing a platform for ultra-high-resolution whole-transcriptome and proteome profiling.

- [**An integrative spatial multi-omic workflow for unified analysis of tumor tissue (SMINT)**](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.crmeth.2026.101306) — 2026-02-12, *Cell Reports Methods*

  `multi-omics integration` `cell segmentation` `spatial metabolomics` `spatial transcriptomics` `workflow`

  Presents SMINT (Spatial Multi-omics INTegration), a computational workflow combining spatial transcriptomics and spatial metabolomics with cell segmentation and coordinate registration to a common reference frame. Applied to IDH-mutated glioma, identifies distinct cellular neighborhoods at the infiltrating edge enriched in multinucleated and oligodendrocyte-lineage tumor cells, associated with differentially abundant metabolites. Reveals that nuclei-only vs. morphology-based segmentation strategies produce complementary information for cell-type annotation.
